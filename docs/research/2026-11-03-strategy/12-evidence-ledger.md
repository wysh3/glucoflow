## What could not be verified

- Private clinic and nursing-home counts and bed-size distribution.
- NABL lab count; IRDAI health premium and claim volumes.
- Tier 1/2/3 and rural patient-volume splits.
- Actual vendor paid seats, churn and realised prices.
- NHM/state tender contract values.
- ABDM and PM-JAY digital funding lines.
- An Indian measurement of ordered tests never reviewed.
- A national NPCDCS/NTEP loss-to-follow-up rate with a published numerator and denominator.
- A multi-site patient-level referral completion figure from PHC to cancer centre.
- A private multi-site OPD no-show rate; only single-site dental and surgical audits.
- Whether the Seoni baseline generalises beyond a QI site chosen for poor performance.
- The current ABDM Health Data Management Policy version and whether the EHR Standards 2016 have been superseded.
- The Data Protection Board appointment status after 30 October 2026.
- State-by-state Clinical Establishments Act adoption.

## The rule for the deck

Every number on a slide gets its source, year and setting in the speaker notes. If a number is an estimate, say so. If a claim is unmeasured, say "target" or "to be measured". A judge who catches one invented number will discount the rest.
