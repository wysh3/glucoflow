# GlucoFlow blueprint compliance audit

## Review basis

Reviewed all 26 pages of the supplied *GlucoFlow Product, Novelty & UX Blueprint*, version 3.0, dated 7 November 2026. The supplied PDF has SHA-256 `56cb9890ecd648a872818b61b3c59cc6a24563397cc4ed1b2350482ae1d17054`. I extracted each page directly from the PDF, matched the earlier extraction byte for byte, and inspected rendered thumbnails for figures and layout. Page numbers below refer to the PDF pages, including its cover.

This is a baseline audit of the source and the previously deployed synthetic care release on branch `codex/hermes-care-workspace`, before the follow-up fixes. It covers the active care workspace, its shared navigation and relevant retained record surfaces. The [care verification record](care-verification-2026-11-07.md) and [deployment record](care-deployment-2026-11-07.md) establish the earlier measured results. This audit used no paid model calls, deployment or device test. Follow-up work needs fresh evidence for its own release.

**Implemented** means the stated capability exists at its specified synthetic MVP level, or that an editorial constraint is applied. **Partial** means a material component is demonstrable and a material requirement remains open. **Not implemented** covers a missing MVP requirement or external validation activity. **Roadmap** covers work explicitly deferred by the PDF or gated on later evidence. One page can contain several classifications.

## Main findings

1. **Genuine Hermes, limited operational authority.** The pinned upstream `AIAgent` calls two care tools and chooses among supported intents. Fastify validates returned references and owns approval and state transitions. Hermes model memory, skill discovery and general-purpose tools are disabled. The app persists chat and simulator state. The active work is one encounter/order evidence monitor, with reconciliation when the app reads the workspace or runs a command. [Hermes runtime](../hermes/runtime.py#L54) · [Hermes policy](../hermes/policy.py#L9) · [Bridge](../apps/api/src/care/chat.ts#L54) · [Repository](../apps/api/src/care/repository.ts#L40)
2. **The current learning exercise governs a display preference.** A human or Hermes can draft an owner-first or source-first handover layout, exercise real sandbox checks, approve the new version and restore a prior version. The version can affect later handovers for multiple synthetic patients in the workspace. There is no structured staff correction, generalized operational procedure, separate approving identity, learned routing or action on a second patient, unit publication or post-release outcome feedback. An encounter count, a version badge or two handovers with different ordering do not prove that workflow. [Playbook schema](../apps/api/src/care/schema.ts#L47) · [Playbook implementation](../apps/api/src/care/playbooks.ts#L27) · [Hermes proposal policy](../hermes/policy.py#L214)
3. **Collective intelligence requires separately attributable collaboration and reuse.** The PDF describes capturing staff corrections and outcomes, reviewing them with designated owners, and publishing a procedure to a limited unit and role. It does not use the term “collective intelligence” or name a patient A/B demo. For the expanded request, the proof should show authenticated person A recording an operational correction against evidence for synthetic patient A, a different authorized person approving a sanitized, patient-independent procedure, and the same approved version helping with synthetic patient B under the relevant unit permission. Retain each person's action and the exact source evidence. Current `owner_id` row-level security and the simulated role selector deliberately model a single authenticated owner, so a browser switching from Nurse to Doctor demonstrates a role exercise only. [Workspace policy](../supabase/migrations/044_care_workspace.sql#L15) · [Simulation role command](../apps/api/src/care/commands.ts#L69) · [Contract](../packages/contracts/src/care.ts#L12)
4. **Episode history and scheduled monitoring are separate requirements.** Care Teams already offers encounter notes, lab orders and an expandable timeline of clickable source events. The app also stores tasks, commands and audit entries. There is no unified episode entry point shared by Ask, Worklist and Activity, and admission/renal context is an array of strings without note-level source references. There is no durable scheduled care job or independent background reconciliation. The previous deployment can scale to zero. The screen label “Last checked” reports the last state or evidence change when a monitor stays unchanged. [Teams episode card](../apps/client/src/features/care/teams.tsx#L47) · [Source drawer](../apps/client/src/features/care/shared.tsx#L149) · [Monitor reducer](../apps/api/src/care/monitor.ts#L29) · [Task details](../apps/client/src/features/care/worklist.tsx#L254) · [Deployment](care-deployment-2026-11-07.md#L31)
