7 November 2026, India Standard Time. This inventory compares page 22 of the 26-page *GlucoFlow Product, Novelty & UX Blueprint* with the working tree, dated deployment evidence, and read-only cloud metadata. It covers the public synthetic demo. Institutional approval, legal applicability, and ABDM certification require separate determinations. [Page-by-page blueprint audit](pdf-compliance-audit-2026-11-07.md) · [Notified DPDP Rules and commencement clauses](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)

## Current scope

GlucoFlow reads uploaded PDFs and images, locally recorded notes and approved facts, and synthetic HIS/LIS events. It has no ABHA identity verification, ABDM consent-manager integration, HIU or HIP registration or adapter, FHIR exchange, or ABHA-linked record retrieval. The fixed clinic identifiers and synthetic encounter codes belong to GlucoFlow. An ABHA identifier alone does not grant access to health records. The Ministry describes ABDM as consent-based exchange through the HIECM gateway and calls for sandbox testing and security audit before integration. [Application scope](../../docs/mvp/01-product.md) · [MoHFW ABDM security statement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2152537&lang=2&reg=48) · [NRCeS ABDM FHIR R4 guide](https://www.nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/)

## Shortest path to a sandbox integration

1. Keep the public build synthetic and place the sandbox behind individually provisioned users and clinic permissions. Disable model egress for ABDM material pending documented approval of its purpose and data flow. Select one hospital partner, one intended purpose and one permitted record type. Confirm who will act as the HIU, the source HIP and the data fiduciary or processor. [NRCeS ABDM roles](https://www.nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/) · [ABDM Health Data Management Policy](https://abdm.gov.in/static/media/health_management_policy_bac9429a79.80f74bc3e039c00acd4f.pdf)
2. Build a server-side exchange boundary only after the partner supplies authorized sandbox registration, credentials and endpoints. Verify official consent artifact scope, purpose, expiry and withdrawal at request and delivery. Handle asynchronous callbacks, authentication, duplicate delivery, replay and expired consent. Validate received FHIR bundles against the applicable published profiles, preserve source provenance, and present scoped results for human review. Keep full source payloads and credentials out of logs and general assistant context. [MoHFW ABDM security and sandbox process](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2152537&lang=2&reg=48) · [NRCeS ABDM FHIR R4 guide](https://www.nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/)
3. Before using patient data, verify assignment- and purpose-based access, notice and consent journeys, rights handling, source access audit, retention for originals and derived data, incident response and all processor locations and terms. Obtain the institution’s legal, privacy, security and ABDM sign-offs for the specific deployment. The notified Rules have staged commencement, and government sandbox validation is a distinct gate. [MeitY DPDP Rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf) · [MoHFW ABDM security statement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2152537&lang=2&reg=48)
