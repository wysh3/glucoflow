# ABDM and India deployment readiness

7 November 2026. GlucoFlow is a synthetic prototype with an existing user-upload and human-review workflow. This review checked source code, blueprint pages 22 and 26, the companion 26-page audit, and public official programme and legal material. The user confirmed that the project is unregistered in the ABDM sandbox. This is a source-based readiness assessment. ## Can GlucoFlow fetch documents through ABHA?

**Yes, through an approved ABDM Health Information User integration and a valid patient consent artefact. The current build cannot do it.** An ABHA number and ABHA address serve distinct identification and account functions. Verification or linking lets the system associate the correct patient with the correct local record. The patient manages consent through a PHR application such as the official ABHA app. Participating hospitals and labs acting as Health Information Providers retain the source records, link care contexts and deliver the data authorized for a Health Information User. The Health Information Exchange and Consent Manager routes permission and requests across the network. The ABHA account alone gives access to neither a hospital record nor an institutional user session. Coverage depends on the source HIP participating, linking the care context and holding the requested record type and date range. [NHA HIP/HIU guidelines](https://sandboxcms.abdm.gov.in/uploads/HIP_HIU_Guidelines_f85df336ec_9c57485ed7.pdf) · [MoHFW explanation of federated consent exchange](https://abdm.gov.in/strapicms/uploads/AU_5642_Z2b_V_Ga_f9035b689e.pdf)

### Verified V3 exchange surface

The official OpenAPI files specify sandbox host `https://dev.abdm.gov.in` and production host `https://apis.abdm.gov.in` for HIE-CM. The paths below were checked against those files. No requests were sent to the transaction endpoints. [Gateway V3 OpenAPI](https://sandboxcms.abdm.gov.in/uploads/gateway_1_0d212e29a3.yaml) · [Consent and data-flow V3 OpenAPI](https://sandboxcms.abdm.gov.in/uploads/consent_management_data_flow12_jan_ded6ab978d.yaml)

1. The server obtains an access token using `POST /api/hiecm/gateway/v3/sessions` with the issued `clientId`, `clientSecret` and grant type. Bridge configuration includes the callback URL and enabled service IDs. Confirm exact required headers, token claims and audience against the environment and agreed participant role.
2. An authorized clinician starts a narrowly scoped request using `POST /api/hiecm/consent/v3/request/init`. Record the requester, patient association, purpose, permitted record types, period and permission parameters. Correlate the on-init callback, reconcile through `/api/hiecm/consent/v3/request/status`, and handle the HIU notification for granted, denied, revoked or expired status. Fetch each granted artefact through `POST /api/hiecm/consent/v3/fetch` and validate it before any data request.
3. The HIU calls `POST /api/hiecm/data-flow/v3/health-information/request` with the granted consent ID, a date window within its scope, recipient key material and an allowlisted HTTPS data push URL. The HIE-CM assigns a transaction context. The HIP sends encrypted data directly to the HIU receiver. Authenticate protocol callbacks, correlate each transaction and page, decrypt using the specified key exchange, validate integrity, and acknowledge accepted or failed transfer through the protocol. Treat a request acknowledgement as pending delivery.
4. Start with one linked laboratory report. Validate FHIR R4 document bundles against the sandbox-accepted ABDM IG and retain the HIP, care context, source ID/version, report time and exact evidence references. NRCeS labels 6.5.0 its current published release and serves a 7.0.0 preview. Confirm the profile required for the pilot rather than treating the preview as the certification baseline. Prefer deterministic mapping for typed report and observation values. Where a consented bundle includes an authorized PDF or image attachment, send it through the existing draft/review seam only under the approved processing purpose. [NRCeS published ABDM FHIR IG](https://nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/) · [Diagnostic report profile](https://www.nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/StructureDefinition-DiagnosticReportRecord.html)

Consent, local record access and downstream processing are independent checks. Revocation or expiry must block new data requests and invalidate permissions on cached views and derived artifacts where required. Determine disposal or justified retention of existing copies with the hospital under the artefact, NHA participant terms and applicable law. Keep delivery status, consent status, and clinical publication status distinct. A historical import must never stand in for a fresh HIS/LIS specimen, verified result or acknowledgement signal.

## Privacy law and programme obligations

### DPDP commencement as of 7 November 2026

The Gazette issues for G.S.R. 843(E) and 846(E) are dated **13 November 2025**. Their electronic references and the MeitY portal listing show 14 November. The legal instruments specify publication, one year and eighteen months. Invest India corroborates the 13 November 2026 and 13 May 2027 calendar dates shown here. [Act commencement Gazette](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/267647.pdf) · [Rules Gazette, rule 1](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf) · [MeitY publication and corrigendum index](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Rules-2025-2) · [Invest India timeline](https://static.investindia.gov.in/s3fs-public/2025-12/navigating_india_s_legal_and_regulatory_framework.pdf)

| Date | Act | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 13 November 2025 | Initial definitions, Board and related machinery, including §§1(2), 2, 18–26, 35, 38–43 and 44(1), (3). This is the tranche in force on the review date. | 1, 2 and 17–21 |
| 13 November 2026 | §6(9) and §27(1)(d), concerning registered Consent Managers and the Board's associated function. | 4 |

- ABDM's HIE-CM role is part of the health information exchange. Registration as a statutory DPDP Consent Manager is a distinct role with separate requirements. GlucoFlow's proposed role here is HIU, with any fiduciary or processor capacity determined from the parties' actual purposes, means and obligations. A valid ABDM grant does not provide blanket permission for model processing, training, marketing or unrelated institutional use. [DPDP Act §§2, 6 and 8](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/22037?view_type=browse) · [Rules 4 and 6](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)
- The delayed DPDP tranche does not suspend existing requirements. Assess the IT Act §43A and SPDI Rules for the relevant body corporate and processing relationship. The SPDI categories include medical records, medical history and health conditions. The Rules address notice, collection, security, disclosure, transfer, retention and grievance handling. Section 44(2), which omits §43A, belongs to the later tranche. [IT Act §43A](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/13116/1/it_act_2000_updated.pdf) · [SPDI Rules G.S.R. 313(E)](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_45_76_00001_200021_1517807324077&filename=GSR313E_10511%281%29_0.pdf&type=rule) · [DPDP commencement Gazette](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/267647.pdf)
- CERT-In's §70B directions address covered entities, specified reportable cyber incidents within six hours of noticing or being informed, a point of contact and a rolling 180-day secure ICT logging requirement with Indian jurisdiction language. The FAQ clarifies additional storage outside India and access to logs. Align the incident runbook with these directions and the separate future DPDP breach obligations. ICT log retention, clinical source retention, consent expiry and application data deletion are distinct clocks. [CERT-In directions](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/CERT-In_Directions_70B_28.04.2022.pdf) · [CERT-In FAQ](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/FAQs_on_CyberSecurityDirections_May2022.pdf)
- HDMP 2020 provides ABDM's federated-storage, purpose and retention baseline. Its §26.6 ties transfers to applicable policy and law and does not state the blanket India storage sentence found in §26.6 of the April 2022 **draft**. MoHFW's published explanation still identifies HDMP 2020. DPDP §16 and Rules 13 and 15 separately address applicable transfer restrictions and specified data. Use India-resident processing as the conservative pilot design, obtain current NHA and hospital terms, and verify the actual location of databases, objects, backups, logs, support and model processing before making a residency claim. A single infrastructure region establishes the location of that resource only. [HDMP 2020 §§26.3 and 26.6](https://abdm.gov.in/static/media/health_management_policy_bac9429a79.80f74bc3e039c00acd4f.pdf) · [NHA April 2022 draft](https://abdm.gov.in/strapicms/uploads/Draft_HDM_Policy_April2022_e38c82eee5.pdf) · [MoHFW confirmation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2152537&lang=2&reg=48)

The hospital and qualified Indian privacy counsel need to resolve the applicable roles, notice and lawful basis, participant terms, security, incident contacts, data-subject requests, retention and any sector- or deployment-specific rules before a real-patient pilot. This technical review is neither a regulatory determination nor an approval to process real records.

## Smallest defensible sandbox milestone

1. Confirm the active registration route with NHA. With NHA and a sponsoring hospital, agree the HIU path, applicable M1–M4 scope, callback and security requirements, and which registered HIP will provide a single synthetic diagnostic report. Keep the existing public demo separated.
2. Put a deterministic ABDM adapter behind the API and worker, with a separate secret store, test tenancy, rate-limited callbacks, environment allowlists and consent-scoped persistence. Give each import a source record ID/version, consent ID, verified patient link, transfer ID and audit trail. Retain only the approved minimum. Use a separate versioned authorization and data-lifecycle design before adding a record to any model context.
3. Demonstrate a synthetic V3 request, actual consent grant in the sandbox PHR, encrypted HIP delivery, FHIR validation, provenance display and human review. Exercise denied, expired and revoked consent, duplicate/reordered callbacks, stale state, replay, mismatched patient, forbidden user, missing source and correction. A passing local fixture alone establishes adapter behavior. A recorded ABDM sandbox transaction establishes that specific exchange.
4. Proceed to the prescribed exit and hospital pilot gates after sandbox proof. Describe the current release as a **synthetic care coordination and document-review prototype with an ABDM integration plan**.
