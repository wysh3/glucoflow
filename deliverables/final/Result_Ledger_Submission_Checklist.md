# Submission checklist

## Before the deadline

- Confirm the exact portal deadline and timezone. The programme brief says 7 November 2026, with a one-day ambiguity in the public bundle.
- Confirm the team has a practising doctor, or a final-year medical student or later, as the clinical lead.
- Confirm every team member and role is listed correctly.
- Upload the current brief and deck, not the old `Delta_*` files.
- Replace the placeholder team names in `Result_Ledger_Submission_Answers.md`.
- Open the hosted demo on a clean browser and on a phone. No password.
- Run the three-minute live script once with a timer.
- Record a backup screen capture of the live path.
- Keep the synthetic data label visible in every screenshot.
- Do not use a real patient record, a real phone number or a real lab report.
- Do not claim a pilot, customer, payment, ABDM integration or clinical validation.
