# The 60-second delta. Demo video script.

Runtime 6 minutes 45 seconds. 16:9, 1920 by 1080, 25 fps. Captions burnt in for the judges' safety copy.

One rule for the whole edit. The film is a moral argument, not a screen recording. The doctor is the hero. The AI is the clerk. Every number on screen carries its label: measured, demo, or target.

Every frame that shows the product carries the footer **Synthetic data. Assistive only. Human approval required.** No exceptions, including the title and the close.

---

## Before you shoot

1. The doctor partner speaks their own words. Lines below are prompts, not a script to read flat.
2. Nothing invented. No stock footage, no fake logos, no partner names, no outcome claims. If a number is not sourced, it does not appear.
3. The app is the synthetic cohort. Real patient data never enters the frame, the recordings, or the project files.
4. Record the screen at 1920 by 1080 with the browser at 100 percent zoom, one window, no bookmarks bar, no other tabs.
5. Keep the timer widget visible from the first demo second to the last. It is the film's spine.

---

## The seven beats

| Time | Beat | What the audience feels |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:40 | Cold open | The weight of two minutes |
| 0:40 to 1:40 | The failure | "I have done this" |
| 1:40 to 2:20 | The insight | A new unit, not a new dashboard |
| 2:20 to 4:30 | The demo | It works, and it refuses to guess |
| 4:30 to 5:35 | The proof | Honest measurement, no theatre |
| 5:35 to 6:25 | Why now, why us, why not them | This team, this moment |
| 6:25 to 6:45 | Close | The sentence they repeat |

## 0:00 to 0:40. Cold open

**Purpose.** Sell the two minutes before anything is explained. No product in frame.

| Shot | In | Out | Frame | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1A | 0:00 | 0:05 | Black | A wall clock ticks once. Sound only. |
| 1B | 0:05 | 0:12 | Close, 50mm | A plastic bag of reports lands on a desk. Paper slides. |
| 1C | 0:12 | 0:22 | Medium, doctor at desk | The doctor sits, pulls the bag closer, does not look at camera. |
| 1D | 0:22 | 0:30 | Extreme close | Second hand moves. A pen taps twice. |
| 1E | 0:30 | 0:40 | Wide, empty chair | The patient's chair is still empty. Hold. |

**Voiceover.** "A follow-up visit begins with a reconstruction. The doctor has two minutes. The bag has forty pages. The question is simple. What changed?"

**On-screen text.** From 0:05, bottom left, 11 pt: `Synthetic data. Assistive only. Human approval required.` From 0:12, lower third: `The 60-second delta.` Nothing else. No logo animation.

**Sound.** Clock, paper, room tone. No music yet.

## 0:40 to 1:40. The failure

**Purpose.** Show the miss, not the dashboard. The evidence lands here, spoken plainly.

| Shot | In | Out | Frame | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2A | 0:40 | 0:52 | Over the shoulder | Hands open three reports, a prescription, a phone screen. |
| 2B | 0:52 | 1:02 | Insert, macro | An eGFR line from July: 55, down from 70. A finger passes over it. |
| 2C | 1:02 | 1:12 | Insert | An eye referral letter, still folded, still in the bag. |
| 2D | 1:12 | 1:22 | Medium, doctor to camera | The doctor looks up. Holds. |
| 2E | 1:22 | 1:34 | Wide, corridor | The patient waits on a bench, file on lap. |
| 2F | 1:34 | 1:40 | Insert | Clock. Two minutes gone. |

**Voiceover.** "Three reports. A prescription. A forwarded photograph. The old value is in the bag. Nobody opened it."

**Doctor, to camera, their own words.** Prompt: *"I did not see this. The patient did not bring it. It was in the bag."*

**Voiceover, over 2E.** "A Chennai audit of 3,696 public-programme patients found 11.2 percent could be reliably tracked by phone and address. A nine-clinic government OPD study puts a follow-up visit at about two minutes. An international review finds 6.8 to 62 percent of abnormal results missed."

**On-screen text, three quiet supers, 8 pt, lower left.**
- `11.2% trackable. Chennai audit, 3,696 patients. BMC Infectious Diseases 2018.`
- `About 2 minutes per follow-up. Nine-clinic OPD study. IJCMH 2019.`
- `6.8% to 62% of abnormal results missed. J Gen Intern Med 2012.`

**Sound.** Room tone drops out under the doctor's line. Music enters at 1:02, low, single sustained note.

## 1:40 to 2:20. The insight

**Purpose.** Replace "another timeline" with "the delta". One idea, four words.

| Shot | In | Out | Frame | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3A | 1:40 | 1:52 | Screen, motion graphics | A dense record scrolls past fast, then stops. |
| 3B | 1:52 | 2:04 | Screen | The four blocks build, one per beat: Changed, Unresolved, Conflicts, What we do not know. |
| 3C | 2:04 | 2:14 | Medium, doctor | Doctor reads, nods once, no reaction shot of software. |
| 3D | 2:14 | 2:20 | Insert | Pen lands on paper. Decision. |

**Voiceover.** "The record is not the doctor's problem. The doctor does not need another timeline. The doctor needs the delta. What changed. What is unresolved. What the record cannot tell you."

**On-screen text.** Four words, one at a time, large: `Changed` / `Unresolved` / `Conflicts` / `What we do not know`. Then one line: `The unit is the delta, not the record.`

**Sound.** The single note resolves. Silence on "What the record cannot tell you."

## 2:20 to 4:30. The demo

**Purpose.** The product, unedited in time order, with a visible timer. The judges must see one page, one click to a source, one conflict that stays unresolved, and one draft note.

Record this as a continuous take. Cut only to the doctor's face, never inside a click.

| Shot | In | Out | Screen | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4A | 2:20 | 2:40 | Delta | Open the brief. Timer starts. Seven changes, three unresolved, two conflicts, three gaps. Three decisions. Scroll nothing. |
| 4B | 2:40 | 3:00 | Source | Open the first decision. Click Source. The original page opens with the line highlighted. Close it. |
| 4C | 3:00 | 3:18 | Unresolved | Open the second decision. Show the 12-month interval and the missing later record. |
| 4D | 3:18 | 3:36 | Conflict | Patient says she stopped the statin. The prescription says continue. Both visible. No recommendation. |
| 4E | 3:36 | 3:48 | Full ledger | Open the full ledger. Show the missing page, the partial date and the unit that cannot merge. |
| 4F | 3:48 | 4:08 | Review sheet | Review three decisions. Accept the eGFR change, flag the eye review, ask the patient about the statin. Each decision is saved as a disposition, not a diagnosis. |
| 4G | 4:08 | 4:22 | Visit note | The draft note assembles from the accepted decisions. The doctor copies it; nothing is written to the record. |
| 4H | 4:22 | 4:30 | Timer close-up | The timer stops. 42 seconds. Hold on the number. |

**Voiceover, sparse.** "Same patient. Seven changes. Three unresolved. Two conflicts. Three gaps. Three decisions."
At 4B: "Click the line. The source opens, and the line is highlighted."
At 4C: "The 2025 eye exam asked for a review in twelve months. No later record exists. The product shows the interval and does not call it overdue."
At 4D: "The patient says she stopped the statin after three days. The prescription says continue. Both are shown. The product does not choose."
At 4E: "One page is missing. It says so."
At 4F: "Accept the change. Flag the eye review. Ask about the statin."
At 4H: "Forty-two seconds from open to decision. One case, one run. The pilot measures the rest." [If the take measured a different number, say that number and record one line of context.]

**On-screen text.** From 2:20, a running timer: `00:42` style, top right, 24 pt, monospace. At 4H, a super: `Demo timing, one synthetic case. Not a measured average.` Never say "42 seconds saved".

**Sound.** No music. Interface sounds are off. The voice carries it.

**Note for the editor.** Never speed up a take to reach forty-two seconds. Time the real flow, then record the voiceover to the number you measured. If the take reads 55 seconds, the line says fifty-five and the film says what is true. Read the four counts off the running demo before the voiceover is recorded. If a count differs from this script, the screen wins and the line changes.

## 4:30 to 5:35. The proof

**Purpose.** Buy trust by refusing to oversell. The KPI is a plan, not a result.

| Shot | In | Out | Frame | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5A | 4:30 | 4:42 | Screen | The four KPI names, one per beat, each stamped `TARGET`. |
| 5B | 4:42 | 4:58 | Medium, doctor to camera | The real line, recorded on the day. Not scripted. |
| 5C | 4:58 | 5:12 | Wide, clinic corridor | Staff move past. The clinic is real, the product is synthetic. |
| 5D | 5:12 | 5:24 | Screen | The pilot plan: three clinics, 100 follow-ups, eight weeks. |
| 5E | 5:24 | 5:35 | Insert | A printed protocol on a desk, a pen beside it. |

**Voiceover.** "We are not claiming an outcome. Here is what the pilot measures. Time to brief, under sixty seconds. Delta completeness. Unresolved items closed within seven days. Doctor-reported confidence. Three clinics. One hundred follow-ups. Eight weeks. A manual baseline and the delta arm on the same cases."

**Doctor, to camera.** Prompt, in their words: *"I would use this tomorrow."* Record it once, unscripted. If they will not say it, cut the line and let the pilot plan carry the beat.

**On-screen text.** Four KPI rows, each with `TARGET`. Then: `Not yet measured.` Then: `3 clinics. 100 follow-ups. 8 weeks.`

## 5:35 to 6:25. Why now, why us, why not them

**Purpose.** Three answers, forty-five seconds, no market-size theatre.

| Shot | In | Out | Frame | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6A | 5:35 | 5:48 | Screen, type only | `WHY NOW` with three short lines. |
| 6B | 5:48 | 6:02 | Screen, type only | `WHY NOT THEM` with the record, summary, delta contrast. |
| 6C | 6:02 | 6:14 | Screen, type only | `WHY US` with four short lines. |
| 6D | 6:14 | 6:25 | Medium, technical lead | One sentence to camera, no slide. |

**Voiceover.** "Why now. Extraction is finally good enough for Indian paper, scans and forwarded photographs. The records are digitised but unusable at the point of care. Why not them. An EMR shows you the record. A summary tells the story. A delta tells you what changed, and refuses to guess. Why us. The backend exists. The evidence binding exists. The clinical partner is here. And we ship in days."

**On-screen text.**

- `WHY NOT THEM`: `A record stores.` / `A summary narrates.` / `A delta decides, and refuses to guess.`
- `WHY US`: `Existing backend, web and Android.` / `Evidence binding.` / `Identity firewall.` / `A clinical partner.`

## App screens to record

Record these in one session, same account, same synthetic patient, same window size. Do not re-record a screen after changing the fixture.

1. **Brief.** The hero page. Seven changed, three unresolved, two conflicts, three gaps. Three decisions. Timer visible.
2. **Review sheet.** One decision at a time. Accept the eGFR change, flag the eye review, ask the patient about the statin. Show the state change.
3. **Source.** The original page with the exact line highlighted, reached from the first decision.
4. **Visit note.** Draft assembled from the accepted decisions. Copy it. Nothing is written to the record.
5. **Full ledger.** The four blocks, including the missing page, partial date and unmergeable unit.
6. **Judge tour, optional.** The 4-minute tour, used only if the live demo fails on the day.

Screen list for the editor: `01_brief`, `02_review`, `03_source`, `04_note`, `05_full_ledger`, `06_tour`.

## Shots the team must film

These cannot be screen-recorded. Book them in one morning, one room, one doctor.

| Location | Shot | Why it earns its place | Props and permissions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consulting room | Doctor at desk, bag of reports, sitting down | The cold open and the close | Desk, chair, report bag, wall clock, consent from the doctor |
| Consulting room | Hands opening reports, close insert | The reconstruction is manual labour | Three reports, one prescription, one folded referral |
| Consulting room | Doctor to camera, two lines | A doctor judge trusts a doctor | Two takes maximum, their own words |
| Clinic corridor | Patient waiting on a bench, file on lap | The two minutes belong to the patient | Consent from the clinic, no identifiable patients |
| Clinic corridor | Staff walking past, shallow focus | Proves a real clinic, not a set | Wide lens, no faces in focus |
| Patient waiting area | Empty chair, then the patient sits | The visit starts before the room | Chairs, daylight, no signage with patient names |
| Desk | Clock, second hand, pen tap | Time as a character | Any analogue clock, consistent across takes |
| Desk | Printed protocol, pen | The pilot is a plan, not a slide | One page, no signatures shown |

Never film a real patient record, a real prescription, or a screen with a name. If a document must appear, it is the synthetic fixture, printed.

## Screen recording checklist

**Setup**
- [ ] 1920 by 1080, browser at 100 percent zoom, single window, bookmarks bar hidden.
- [ ] Demo route open before recording starts. No login in frame.
- [ ] Synthetic banner visible on every screen.
- [ ] Timer overlay prepared and tested; the app's 42-second read chip is visible.
- [ ] Notifications off. No other tabs. No personal desktop.
- [ ] Cursor size large enough to read at 1080p, but not enlarged.

**The take**
- [ ] One continuous take from brief to draft note. Cuts only to the doctor.
- [ ] No scrolling to reach the three decisions. The brief is one page.
- [ ] Every click completes before the voiceover names it.
- [ ] The source panel closes before the next decision is opened.
- [ ] The conflict screen is held for at least two seconds with no recommendation on screen.
- [ ] The full ledger gap block is shown even if the take runs long.

**After**
- [ ] Record the real elapsed time from open to draft note. Write it down. Use that number, not the planned one.
- [ ] Export the recording, name it `delta_demo_take<n>_<date>.mov`.
- [ ] Watch it once at 1x with sound off. If a step is not legible without narration, re-record the step.
- [ ] Keep the take as the offline fallback for the live demo.

## Assembly and quality control

- **Captions.** Burn in English. Two lines maximum. Never cover the timer, the synthetic banner, or the page number.
- **Loudness.** Voice at minus 16 LUFS, true peak minus 1. Music, where it exists, sits 18 dB under the voice and ducks further under the doctor.
- **Colour.** Match the product palette. Canvas `#f5f8f5`, ink `#203b38`, primary `#26796e`, attention `#a85b00`. Nothing else.
- **Type on screen.** Inter. Titles 48 pt and up. Supers 24 pt minimum. Sources 18 pt minimum, on screen for at least three seconds.
- **Runtime.** Locked at 6:45. If a beat overruns, cut from the demo before cutting from the proof or the close.
- **Numbers.** Every number on screen has its label: measured, demo, or target. One number per claim.
- **Names.** No real patient, doctor, clinic, or partner name appears without written consent. The synthetic patient stays labelled.
- **Export.** H.264, 1920 by 1080, 25 fps, AAC 192 kbps. Deliver as `Delta_Demo_6m45s.mp4`.

---

## Do not

- Do not open with a dashboard tour or a title animation longer than two seconds.
- Do not say "AI doctor", "AI triage", "diagnosis", "risk score", or "saves lives".
- Do not claim a measured saving. The pilot is the measurement.
- Do not show the old Glucoflow screens, the old pitch, or any earlier product name.
- Do not fabricate a doctor quote, a partner logo, or a pilot result.
- Do not show a real record, a real prescription, or a patient's face without consent.
- Do not let the timer stop on a number the take did not earn.
