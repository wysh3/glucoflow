# glucoflow launch video

**Format:** 1920x1080, 30 fps, 20 seconds, H.264 + AAC.
**Tone:** calm, clinical, confident. No hype, no diagnosis, no fake urgency.

## Storyboard

| Time | Scene | What is on screen | Copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0-2.5 | The stack | A stack of synthetic lab, prescription and patient-note cards | 40 pages. Two minutes. A follow-up visit starts with a stack. |
| 2.5-6.0 | The product | Real landing page, cursor moves to the demo button | One page before the patient sits down. |
| 6.0-9.5 | The decisions | Real delta screen; eGFR card highlighted; cursor | Three decisions. No scrolling. |
| 9.5-13.0 | The evidence | Real source drawer; exact quoted line highlighted | Every line opens its source. |
| 13.0-16.5 | The assist | Live GPT-6 Luna guide panel; source card visible | AI reads the record. You decide. |
| 16.5-18.5 | The body | Real interactive 3D anatomy viewer | A body map that opens. |
| 18.5-20.0 | End card | glucoflow wordmark, URL, synthetic-data line | glucoflow.vercel.app |

## Production

- Captured real UI at 1920x1080 from the working app and the hosted site.
- The 3D body is the real Three.js viewer and the same CC BY 4.0 Visible Human Female reference used in the product.
- Motion is built as a 20-second HTML timeline and recorded in Chrome, not screen-recorded by hand.
- Music: "Happy Beats Business Moves Vol. 10" by Ende Dot App, from the brag asset pack, trimmed and faded to 20 seconds.
- SFX: soft impact on cuts, one click on the source action.
- Audio is limited to -16 LUFS integrated, -1.5 dBTP.
- The body is a reference anatomy, not a patient scan. All records are synthetic.
