# glucoflow launch video

**Format:** 1920x1080, 30 fps, 20 seconds, H.264 + AAC.
**Tone:** calm, administrative, exact. No hype, no diagnosis, no clinical claim.

## Storyboard

| Time | Scene | What is on screen | Copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0-3.0 | The result | Three synthetic reports with no owner, no clock and no receipt | A report arrives. Nobody owns it. |
| 3.0-7.0 | The product | Real public landing page | Every result gets an owner, a clock and a receipt. |
| 7.0-12.0 | The ledger | Real clinic result ledger, critical alert visible | Ten open. Two critical. Who owns the result? |
| 12.0-15.8 | The receipt | Real receipt drawer with source, flags, clock and audit trail | One owner. One clock. One proof. |
| 15.8-18.0 | The proof | Public receipt verifier, chain head visible | A code anyone can verify. |
| 18.0-20.0 | End card | glucoflow wordmark, URL, synthetic-data line | glucoflow.vercel.app |

## Production

- Captured real UI at 1920x1080 from the working app and the hosted site.
- Motion is a 20-second HTML timeline recorded in Chrome.
- Music: "Happy Beats Business Moves Vol. 10" by Ende Dot App, trimmed and faded to 20 seconds.
- SFX: soft impact on cuts, one click on the receipt action.
- Audio is limited to -16 LUFS integrated, -1.5 dBTP.
- The lab flag is copied from the seeded report. The ledger does not interpret it. All records are synthetic.
